About us

We are a dedicated team that values your time and investment, offering reliable post-project support. Our developers use modern technologies and tools to build high-quality apps with robust databases and effective design solutions.

Job Description

We are looking for a passionate Flutter Developer with 2–4 years of experience to join our team in Bhopal. You will be responsible for building high-performance, scalable cross-platform mobile applications using Flutter and Dart, working closely with designers and backend developers to deliver outstanding user experiences.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop, test, and deploy cross-platform mobile applications using Flutter & Dart
  • Collaborate with UI/UX designers to implement clean and user-friendly interfaces
  • Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code
  • Integrate RESTful APIs and third-party libraries
  • Debug and fix issues to ensure optimal performance

Requirements

  • 2–4 years of professional experience in Flutter development
  • Strong knowledge of Dart language
  • Experience with state management solutions (e.g., Provider, Bloc, Riverpod)
  • Familiarity with REST APIs, JSON, and third-party integrations
  • Good understanding of mobile app architecture

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work on live projects with modern technologies
  • Friendly and collaborative work environment
  • Competitive salary as per industry standard
  • Alternate 2nd and 4th Saturday Off
